How to Start

It is 2147. You run a corporation. You hire staff, lease mining rights on a planet, extract what is in it, and sell it on the Galactic Exchange, where the other side of an order may be another corporation run by another player. Everything you build persists between sessions. Nothing here is a race: progress is measured in milestones, not minutes.

Before you begin

  • Register a corporation. It is issued $250,000 and docked at Earth Station Prime. Your whole first session happens there.
  • Use a desktop browser. The interface is built for one.
  • Elara Voss of the Contracting & Assignments Bureau will offer to brief you. Accept, and she takes you through the steps below in this order. Decline, and this page is the same procedure. Your settlement licence is in your Inbox either way.

The first hour, in eight steps

  1. Rent an office. Station → Orbital Executive Suites. Suites cost $1,000 a day; the shortest term is 7 days, $7,000. Without an active office the ISA files no lease, hires no staff, and starts no mining cycle.
  2. Hire five employees. The Workforce panel of the same suites. Each hire costs $2,000 and adds $150 a day to payroll. The ISA requires 5 employees on payroll for each mining lease you hold.
  3. File a mining lease. Station → ISA Claims & Leases Division. A station files leases only on the body it orbits, so from Earth Station Prime the lease is Earth, Silicate Extraction, $20,000.
  4. Commission an extractor yard. In the same office, open the lease with Manage Lease and build a Basic Extractor Yard: $50,000. A lease without equipment is paperwork.
  5. Start a mining cycle. Set a throughput between 10 and 250 tons per hour and a duration of up to 72 hours. Silicates accrue to your Earth Station Prime inventory while it runs. Throughput costs more per ton the higher you set it: the top setting is not the most profitable one.
  6. Sell what you mined. Galactic Exchange. A Standing Buy Order from the GEX Commodities Authority takes Silicates at once, at about $24 a ton when the market is quiet; the price falls as the day's quota fills and recovers overnight. The Exchange keeps 8% of every sale. You may instead list them for other corporations, and wait for a buyer.
  7. Fund your first research. Corporate R&D → Basic Extraction Analytics: 2 hours, $2,000, and a permanent +10% to extraction throughput on every yard.
  8. Take a contract, then read your milestones. Assignments is the Bureau: agents issue contracts with a quota and a reward, and no deadline. Then open the Overview, where the ISA lists what your next corporation level requires. When you do not know what to do, start there.

If something breaks

Post it in Forums → Help & Guides with a title that begins Bug:, and tell the person who invited you. Say what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and roughly when. Never post your password or your email address; nobody needs either to fix a bug.

Not in this build

  • Corporation levels above 10. Ten are defined; 11 to 40 are planned for a later version.
  • Combat between corporations. It is planned, deliberately late.
  • Anything bought with real money. Nothing is for sale, and nothing will be before 1.0.
  • Moderation. Chat, forums and mail are not filtered, and there is no report, block or mute. Everything players write is shown as they typed it.
