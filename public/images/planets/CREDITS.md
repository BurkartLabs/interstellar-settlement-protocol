# Planet and moon images: sources and licences

Every image here is NASA imagery in the public domain (AB#741). NASA's media usage guidelines say NASA content is
generally not subject to copyright in the United States, and JPL's image use policy says its images may be used for
any purpose unless noted otherwise. None of these is noted otherwise: the images.nasa.gov record of each was read
on 2026-09-22, and none credits a foreign agency (ESA, DLR) or carries a Creative Commons licence, which the
catalogue marks where it applies. Images credited to a US university or institute worked on NASA missions under
NASA; the credit line below is the one NASA asks for.

- NASA media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
- JPL image use policy: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy

Each file is the NASA image cropped square around the disc, scaled to 512 x 512 and saved as JPEG (under 150 KB).
NASA's logo and insignia are not used, and nothing here implies NASA endorses the game.
