"Humanity did not conquer space. It privatized it."

ISP is a long-horizon online strategy simulation where you operate as CEO of a corporate colonization firm navigating industrial logistics, legal conflict, interstellar finance, and deterministic warfare at a civilizational scale. A true slow-burn universe — level progression is milestone-based and full corporate maturity unfolds over years of real-world play.

Persistent multiplayer state Milestone progression, not XP grind Text-first command interface

What ISP Is

Persistent Economic Civilization

Every resource chain, market order, and investment contributes to a persistent economy.

Corporate Power Strategy

Scale your corporation through hiring, infrastructure, R&D, and sector expansion.

Hybrid Conflict Layer

Compete militarily, politically, and financially across multiple star systems.

Core Gameplay Pillars

Milestone Progression

Corporation Level 1-40 is unlocked through business milestones: first refined output, first off-world mining, employee targets, R&D completions, and strategic expansion checkpoints.

Industrial Refinement Chains

Raw materials become strategic products through multi-step pipelines gated by Corporate R&D. Supply chain planning and throughput optimization become long-term differentiators.

Financial Command

Run full corporate finance: cash flow, liabilities, valuation, debt instruments, bond strategy, and inter-corporate investment plays driven by performance and risk.

Conglomerate Politics

Join or build alliances with expanding membership caps, pooled resources, shared intel, station co-ownership, and coordinated policy pressure.

Map, Warfare, And Discovery

System-Scale Starmap

Navigate from cluster view to system and body-level detail for strategic planning.

Deterministic Combat Core

Outcomes are numbers-driven with counters, modifiers, and doctrine effects.

Scouting Intelligence

Resource composition is unknown until surveyed, creating real exploration pressure.

Economic Engine In Detail

Raw Resource Genesis

Every new corporation begins in a raw-material economy. Silicates, metals, isotopes, and volatile compounds become the industrial substrate for every advanced product in the game.

Refining As Competitive Edge

Refineries are not decoration. Throughput, downtime control, and process sequencing determine your cost basis and export strength as regional demand shifts.

Market Signals And Arbitrage

Use local scarcity and congestion to set pricing, absorb volatility, and arbitrage between systems while balancing freight risk and liquidity windows.

Capital And Leverage

Scale by issuing debt, taking strategic loans, and investing in rival corporations. Your capital strategy can outperform brute extraction alone.

Long-Horizon Campaign Structure

Phase I: Establishment

Build initial facilities, stabilize payroll, and secure first extraction contracts.

Phase II: Industrial Scale

Expand refining chains, automate logistics, and begin controlled fleet growth.

Phase III: Sector Influence

Enter legal and political contests for tariff regimes, patent constraints, and territorial leverage.

Phase IV: Conglomerate Politics

Coordinate with allied corporations to project force, protect trade lanes, and co-own key stations.

Phase V: Interstellar Governance

Compete in system-wide influence wars where law, capital, and fleet math decide civilization-scale outcomes.

Designed For Years, Not Weeks

ISP intentionally resists short-loop progression so strategic planning and institutional momentum matter.

Why This Prototype Exists

Accessible, Text-First Core

Gameplay systems are intentionally text-dominant for readability, speed, and accessibility with or without heavy visuals.

Expandable Architecture

Current modules are structured so economy, warfare, diplomacy, and missions can be expanded into full production domains.

Real-Time Multiplayer Backbone

Market, communication, and conflict signals are already shaped for live state synchronization and persistent world updates.

Strategic Depth Over Flash

The prototype prioritizes decision quality and long-term outcomes over superficial visual noise.
