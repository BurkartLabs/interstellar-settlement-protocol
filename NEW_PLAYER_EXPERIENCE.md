# New Player Experience

## Initial Walkthrough

When a player first joins the game, they will be in a guided walkthrough starting on their mailbox, highlighting a message they had just received from the "Interstellar Settlement Authority" granting them a license to begin off-world operations. 

After the player opens the message, they are then brought through the various options on the UI. Some options will be unavailable until they rent their first office, which will be the next step of the walkthrough.

The player is guided finally to the starmap screen. This shows nearby star systems, planets in those systems, moons of those planets, stations on those moons or planets, asteroid belts, etc. 

The player is guided to a station and shown how to rent an office.

To start, the player's corporation has $250,000, no assets, and no employees; thus they have no operating expenses. Renting an office costs $1000/day and have a minimal rental period of 30 days. Employees cost $2,000 to hire, and cost $150/day. 

Now that the player has an office, and some employees, now the corporation needs something to do that makes the company money. To start, that will be mining silicates with a "Basic Extractor Yard". 

To build a basic extractor yard, the corporation will need a mining lease on a suitable planet. The walkthrough will navigate the player to the "ISA Claims & Leases Division" to purchase a mining lease for Mars. Mining leases have enough space (to start) for 2 buildings. For each mining lease purchased, you need at least 5 employees. If you have 23 employees, you can purchase up to 4 mining leases. 

After this, the walkthrough then guides the player to their new mining lease to build a Basic Extractor Yard, which costs $50,000 + logistics fees to assemble. Then, they will be shown how to start their first mining cycle. 

After the mining cycle starts, the walkthrough will take them to the Corporate R&D Page where they will be guided towards starting research of "Basic Extraction Analysis", which yields a 10% boost to all extraction gains. 

The walkthrough then takes the player to the Galactic Exchange in order to sell their hard earned silicates to one of the buy orders listed by an NPC Corporation. 

At this point, they have completed the basic intro to the game. The walkthrough tells them that they now know the basics, and to keep researching, expanding, and working on Corporation Milestones. It then takes them to the Missions page, and suggests accepting a mission as the next step. From here, the first walkthrough ends, until later on in the game.
