# Tier 4: the final five resources, one tier table, and the Tier 4 recipes (ISP-184, AB#878)

v0.11 is "Tier 4 Production and Last 5 Resources" (DEVELOPMENT_PHASES.md): *"High-tier exotic resources with R&D gates
(Exotic Matter, Uranium, and remaining catalogue entries)"*, *"Tier 4 manufacturing chains and advanced product
synthesis"*, *"Final 5 resource types fully integrated into extraction, refinery, and market systems"*. Every later
v0.11 story (AB#879 to AB#887) names items from this file, so it fixes which five resources, one tier numbering for
everything, the research that gates them, the Tier 4 recipes and where each product is used.

It is a design, read against ISP main `1cef8326`, 2026-09-27, and it builds on v0.8's two
(`docs/design/tier3-resources.md`, AB#822; `docs/design/tier3-recipes.md`, AB#823): v0.8's rule for prices, its bids
and quotas, and its stages are kept, and nothing here is in `data/` yet. Every number is **chosen, not derived**; the
stories that put it into data model it first, as ECONOMY.md does. The arithmetic below is reproduced by
`tests/unit/tier4Design.test.js` from this file's own tables and the build's Tier 3 bids.

**Prior art** is EVE Online's Planetary Industry: raw R0 resources become P1 to P4 products, and a P4 item needs inputs
that come from several planet types ([EVE University, Planetary Industry](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Planetary_Industry)).
v0.8 already took its stages (P1 to P3 here are stages 1 to 3). Tier 4 takes the last step: a Tier 4 product needs two
intermediates from different tracks **and** an exotic resource that only a few places in the galaxy give.

## 1. Which five

The plan names two, Exotic Matter and Uranium, and "remaining catalogue entries". The catalogue (`data/items.json`) has
16 raw items. v0.8 put 14 of them to work: Water Ice and Hydrogen feed `cryogenics-chain`, Thorium feeds
`thorium-fuel-chain`, Cobalt and Lithium feed `magnet-chain` and `battery-chain` (AB#830 to AB#833). **Only Uranium and
Exotic Matter feed nothing**; there are no remaining unused catalogue entries. So the final five are those two and
three new ones:

| id | Name | Tier | New? | How it is obtained | Where | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `uranium` | Uranium | 4 | no | **extracted** from belts | the eight belts that carry it today (tc-cloud 8%, ee-crown 12%, l726-belt 10%, kap-drift 18%, ei-belt 10%, w1061-reach 8%, o70-belt 12%, vm-field 20%) | `tt-actinide-handling` |
| `exotic-matter` | Exotic Matter | 4 | no | **extracted** from belts | the seven belts that carry it today, all in exotic systems but pr-scatter (Procyon, rare) (ee-crown 8%, pr-scatter 8%, kap-drift 14%, ei-belt 12%, kr60-stream 12%, alt-ring 12%, vm-field 22%) | `tt-exotic-containment` |
| `platinum-group-metals` | Platinum-Group Metals | 4 | **yes** | **extracted** from bodies, on a lease | crater and lava planets and moons in **exotic** systems (ee-kestrel, ee-lyra, ei-ba, kr60-i, w1061-b, w1061-c, vm-cinder): 10 of the body's Iron Ore share becomes Platinum-Group Metals | `tt-deep-core-extraction` |
| `metallic-hydrogen` | Metallic Hydrogen | 4 | **yes** | **extracted** from bodies, on a lease | gas giants (bands) in **exotic** systems (ee-orion, ei-ab, o70-a1): 10 of the body's Hydrogen share becomes Metallic Hydrogen, drawn from the deep layers | `tt-deep-atmosphere-extraction` (and v0.8's skimming research, `tt-fusion-fuel-cycle`) |
| `antimatter` | Antimatter | 4 | **yes** | **synthesized**, never mined | the Synthesis Array (section 4): `antimatter-chain` | `tt-antimatter-confinement` |

**What the gate does.** Without the node, the item's share is not recovered: an expedition to kap-drift brings back its
other items as today and no Uranium, and a lease on ee-kestrel yields its v0.8 profile with the Platinum-Group Metals
share lost. With the node, the share is recovered. No existing belt composition changes (they are pinned by
tests/unit/systemsCatalog.test.js), and a body's share moves the way v0.8's rarity rule already moves one
(tier3-resources.md, section 3). AB#882 builds the gate; AB#879 adds the three new items.

The two **new extracted** items come from bodies, not belts, and only in exotic systems: going far is how a corporation
gets to Tier 4, which is what v0.7's twenty systems were for.

## 2. One tier table

A tier says **how hard an item is to get** (v0.8's rule, tier3-resources.md section 1). Tier 4 is the first tier
defined by its gate: everything behind Tier 4 research, whether extracted in a few exotic places or made from them.

| Tier | Meaning | How it is obtained | Items |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1, common raw** | extracted; available in Sol | leases on Sol bodies, Sol's belt and belts past Sol | Silicates, Helium-3, Nickel, Carbon, Water Ice, Hydrogen, Deuterium, Iron Ore, Bauxite |
| **2, scarce raw** | extracted; not in Sol, or only in trace amounts | belts past Sol (weighted by rarity); a few bodies in rare and exotic systems | Titanium, Rare Earths, Lithium, Cobalt, Thorium |
| **3, refined** | made in a Refinery from Tier 1 to 3 items, stages 1 to 3 | `data/refinery-chains.json` | the 25 v0.8 products (tier3-recipes.md, section 4) |
| **4, exotic** | behind Tier 4 research: extracted in exotic places, or made from what is | section 1's five; section 3's products, in the Synthesis Array | Uranium, Exotic Matter, Platinum-Group Metals, Metallic Hydrogen, Antimatter; Superconducting Coils, Field Emitters, Breeder Reactor Cores, Antimatter Drives, Catalyst Beds |

This is the numbering `data/items.json`'s `tier` field uses from AB#879. It replaces the plan's "Tier 3 Resources"
(v0.8) and "Tier 4 Production" headings and LORE.md's three tiers with one list. **Two items move: Uranium and Exotic
Matter go from Tier 2 to Tier 4** (tests/unit/tier3Resources.test.js pins them at 2 and changes with AB#879).

## 3. The Tier 4 recipes

Tier 4 recipes run in the **Synthesis Array**, not a Refinery (section 4). Each product takes two Tier 3 intermediates
from different v0.8 tracks and one exotic, so every product needs three tracks and one exotic source (EVE's P4 shape).
Bases follow v0.8's rule (tier3-recipes.md, section 1): **at base prices a cycle's outputs are worth 1.15 times its
inputs**, `base = round(1.15 × inputs at base ÷ units out)`, and a floor is 55% of base.

| Chain | Stage | Inputs per cycle | Outputs per cycle | Cycle | Research | Tracks drawn on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `antimatter-chain` | 2 | Exotic Matter 5 + Fusion Pellets 20 | Antimatter 2 | 24 h | `tt-antimatter-confinement` | Fuel + exotic |
| `superconductor-chain` | 2 | Rare-Earth Magnets 10 + Cryogenic Coolant 20 + Metallic Hydrogen 10 | Superconducting Coils 5 | 10 h | `tt-deep-atmosphere-extraction` | Rare + Fuel + exotic |
| `field-emitter-chain` | 2 | Dark-Matter Capacitors 4 + Sensor Arrays 6 + Exotic Matter 2 | Field Emitters 3 | 12 h | `tt-exotic-containment` | Fuel + Rare + exotic |
| `breeder-core-chain` | 4 | Compact Fission Modules 4 + Pressure Vessels 10 + Uranium 20 | Breeder Reactor Cores 4 | 16 h | `tt-actinide-handling` | Metals + Rare + exotic |
| `antimatter-drive-chain` | 4 | Ion Thrusters 4 + Lightweight Spaceframes 6 + Antimatter 1 | Antimatter Drives 3 | 20 h | `tt-antimatter-confinement` | Fuel + Rare + Light + Metals + exotic |
| `catalyst-bed-chain` | 2 | Catalyst Substrates 10 + Graphene Lattice 20 + Platinum-Group Metals 5 | Catalyst Beds 5 | 8 h | `tt-deep-core-extraction` | Rare + carbon + exotic |

Each Tier 4 chain also needs `tt-integrated-manufacturing` (v0.8's cross-track node), which the Synthesis Array's own
gate requires (section 5).

(tier3-recipes.md, section 4): what one facility makes of it in a day, `24 ÷ cycle hours × units per cycle`, rounded.

### The engine already takes several inputs

The acceptance for this story says the refinery engine is single-input (`input`, `inputQuantityPerCycle`), so that
v0.11-04 must generalise it. **That is no longer so.** Since v0.8 (AB#823, AB#825), every chain in
`data/refinery-chains.json` has an `inputs` list debited together or not at all, and `fusion-core-chain` and
`ion-thruster-chain` already take three inputs. What Tier 4 needs from the engine instead is **a second building that
runs chains**: a chain names the building it runs in (`building`: `refinery` or `synthesis-array`). **For the PM:**
AB#881 (ISP-187, "Let refinery chains take more than one input") is done by v0.8 as written; it should be re-scoped to
"a chain names its building", or closed and folded into AB#883.

## 4. The Synthesis Array: why allocation has an interior optimum (T-018)

All six Tier 4 chains run in one new player building, the **Synthesis Array** (`synthesis-array`, AB#883): one per
corporation (`cap.perCorporation: 1`), gated by `tt-antimatter-confinement`'s prerequisite `tt-high-energy-synthesis`
(section 5). One building means **24 hours a day to share among six recipes**, and two kinds of competition:

- **For facility time.** Every Tier 4 chain queues on the same Array.
- **For the same intermediate.** `superconductor-chain` and v0.8's `ion-thruster-chain` (whose Ion Thrusters feed
  `antimatter-drive-chain`) both consume Rare-Earth Magnets; `antimatter-chain` and v0.8's `fusion-core-chain` both
  consume Fusion Pellets.

**A worked example.** One Array's day, split between Superconducting Coils (10 h a cycle, 5 units, inputs worth 6,440
at base) and Field Emitters (12 h, 3 units, inputs worth 4,782). Each product sells into its own standing bid, which
walks down as it is sold into (`npcBidFor`: 50% lower at a full day's quota, never below its 55% floor). Inputs are
valued at base, and every figure is credits for that day:

| Coils cycles | Emitters cycles | Hours used | Coils sold for | Emitters sold for | Inputs | **Net** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 1 | 12 | 0 | 5,041 | 4,782 | **259** |
| 0 | 2 | 24 | 0 | 8,707 | 9,564 | **−857** |
| 1 | 0 | 10 | 6,788 | 0 | 6,440 | **348** |
| 2 | 0 | 20 | 12,033 | 0 | 12,880 | **−847** |
| **1** | **1** | **22** | 6,788 | 5,041 | 11,222 | **607** |

Neither "all Coils" nor "all Emitters" is best: the second cycle of either sells into a bid its first cycle has already
pushed down, below what its inputs are worth. **One of each** nets 607, more than either alone, and leaves two hours
idle rather than run a losing cycle. The optimum moves with every bid and every input price, so it is a choice a
corporation makes again each day, not a rule (T-018). A second Tier 4 recipe, or selling the intermediates raw, is the
same decision with more rows. These are the design's own formulas at base prices, not play; ISP-193's probe (AB#887)
is what shows whether a bot's allocation lands inside the range (P-PROXY).

**As built (ISP-189, AB#883).** The six chains are in `data/refinery-chains.json` with `"building": "synthesis-array"`; a chain without one runs in a Refinery, and a facility runs only its own kind (the start route refuses the other). The Array keeps its own list (`corp.synthesisArrays`) beside `corp.refineries`, so a Refinery Complex still upgrades the n-th Refinery, and both run through one cycle engine (`server/game/refining.js`). It is built through `POST /gameplay/build`, one per corporation, behind `tt-high-energy-synthesis` and `tt-integrated-manufacturing`. Its price, 200,000 (asset value 140,000), is chosen here, since this file names none (ECONOMY.md). That is AB#881's "a chain names its building".

## 5. Research

Six nodes, each tier 4 or 5 in `data/research.json` (its tiers already run to 5). Costs sit with the build's own tier 4
nodes (20,000 to 35,000) and its one tier 5 node (60,000).

| Node | Name | Tier | Needs | Cost | Hours | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tt-actinide-handling` | Actinide Handling | 4 | `tt-rare-metal-separation` | 30,000 | 24 | Uranium's share of an expedition; `breeder-core-chain` |
| `tt-deep-core-extraction` | Deep-Core Extraction | 4 | `tt-ferrous-metallurgy`, `tt-long-baseline-navigation` | 30,000 | 24 | Platinum-Group Metals on exotic crater and lava leases; `catalyst-bed-chain` |
| `tt-deep-atmosphere-extraction` | Deep-Atmosphere Extraction | 4 | `tt-fusion-fuel-cycle`, `tt-long-baseline-navigation` | 30,000 | 24 | Metallic Hydrogen on exotic gas-giant leases; `superconductor-chain` |
| `tt-high-energy-synthesis` | High-Energy Synthesis | 4 | `tt-integrated-manufacturing` | 36,000 | 28 | the Synthesis Array (AB#883) |
| `tt-exotic-containment` | Exotic Containment | 5 | `tt-containment-physics`, `tt-exotic-energy-routing`, `tt-frontier-cartography` | 45,000 | 32 | Exotic Matter's share of an expedition; `field-emitter-chain` |
| `tt-antimatter-confinement` | Antimatter Confinement | 5 | `tt-exotic-containment`, `tt-high-energy-synthesis` | 60,000 | 36 | `antimatter-chain`, `antimatter-drive-chain` |

Every prerequisite named exists in `data/research.json` today, and the test reads them from it. AB#880 adds the nodes.

**Hours.** The acceptance for AB#880 cites 2 to 18 hours per node; the build is past that already, with tier 4 at 20
hours (`tt-integrated-manufacturing`, `tt-long-baseline-navigation`) and its tier 5 node at 28
(`tt-frontier-cartography`). These run 24 to 36 because they are the last nodes in the tree, each needs a tier 4 or 5
node first, and they gate the rarest items in the game: a Tier 4 corporation should have waited for them.

**How the gates are enforced** (AB#880). An extraction node lists its item in `recovers`; `server/game/researchGates.js`
reads that, and expeditions and extractors both drop the item's share without the node. The Synthesis Array and the
Tier 4 chains name their nodes in `researchGates` and `requiresTechIds`, which the build and start routes already check.

## 6. No free lunch: a use for every Tier 4 product besides the bid (P-FREE-LUNCH)

Each product has one use outside the NPC bid, built by AB#886. Each is named by the id it attaches to:

| Product | Use | Attaches to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Superconducting Coils | the Refinery's tier-4 upgrade: 10 Coils beside its credits, for 2.5 times output | `refineryUpgrades` in `data/economy.json`, a new `toTier: 4` row |
| Catalyst Beds | the Assembly Facility's tier-3 upgrade: 20 Catalyst Beds beside its credits | `assemblyFacilityUpgrades` in `data/economy.json`, a new `toTier: 3` row |
| Field Emitters | a Bureau requisition | a new mission template `ms-log-011` in `data/missions.json` (`resource: "Field Emitters"`) |
| Breeder Reactor Cores | a Bureau requisition | a new mission template `ms-log-012` (`resource: "Breeder Reactor Cores"`) |
| Antimatter Drives | a Bureau deep-survey requisition | a new mission template `ms-log-013` (`resource: "Antimatter Drives"`) |
| Antimatter | the input of `antimatter-drive-chain` (section 3) | the chain itself |

Upgrades cost credits only today; a goods cost beside the credits is new, and AB#886 adds it to the upgrade route's
debit (all or nothing, as a chain's inputs are). None of these uses is a v0.10 unit cost: a sixth unit, or a Tier 4
good in an existing unit's cost, would change the combat design's five-way cycle and its tables (v0.10-combat.md,
section 9, "fail if a sixth unit breaks either"), so that is left to a combat decision, not taken here.

**As built (AB#886).** A tier row's `goods` ([{ item, quantity }]) comes from the corporation's stock at the station it
is docked at; undocked, the upgrade is refused ("Dock where you hold them"). The Refinery's tier 4 costs 250,000
credits (asset value 175,000, 12 h offline); the Assembly Facility's tier 3 costs 60,000 (42,000, 4 h, fabrication at
25%). Both prices are chosen, not derived (ECONOMY.md). The three requisitions pay 3,000, 4,250 and 8,000 credits a unit, above
each product's NPC bid base, and are offered only to a corporation with the research that makes the product
(`requiresTechIds`: Exotic Containment, Actinide Handling, Antimatter Confinement). The cross-catalog check now reads
upgrade goods too. Tests: `tests/integration/tier4-uses.test.js`.
