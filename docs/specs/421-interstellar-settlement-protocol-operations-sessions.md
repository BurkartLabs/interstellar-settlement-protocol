# interstellar-settlement-protocol operations: sessions without a story

Feature AB#421 (Proposed) · product interstellar-settlement-protocol · migrated from Azure DevOps on 2026-09-30 (AB#4889)

## What it is

Where it sits: the standing home for work on interstellar-settlement-protocol that has no story of its own — the nightly groom, a depth probe, a Critic sweep, a standup that touches this product. Child of the product epic.
What it is for: Bug #419. Those sessions still open a pull request, and main requires every pull request to link a work item. They used to link the product epic — and Azure DevOps closes every work item linked to a pull request when it is completed, so the first merge closed the epic and took the entire product out of the platform: no product in the Console, and none for the dispatcher to schedule against.
Why this item is safe where the epic was not: nothing depends on its state. If a merge closes it, that is recoverable noise; reopen it, or let it stay closed and the next session links the company item instead. The product is identified by its epic's product-epic tag, which is now read regardless of state, and killed only by stage:Killed — a deliberate act with a name.
This item is not expected to close, and is not a unit of work. It is an address.
